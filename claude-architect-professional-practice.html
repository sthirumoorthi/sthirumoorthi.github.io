ROUND 01 · Architecture

A service-request assistant can use a workflow, RAG, or an autonomous agent. What should guide the choice?

Choose the least complex architecture that meets measured business and risk requirements.

ROUND 02 · Context engineering

How should two model configurations be compared?

A fair evaluation uses the same representative tasks and explicit success measures.

ROUND 03 · Integration

A RAG assistant can create service tickets. Where should approval happen?

Authorization and approval belong in deterministic application controls around writes.

ROUND 04 · Evaluation

What belongs in a release evaluation matrix?

Break results down by task and failure type, using both automated and human checks where needed.

ROUND 05 · Governance

What is the strongest control for confidential documents?

Policies must be enforced in the surrounding system, with accountable audit trails.
