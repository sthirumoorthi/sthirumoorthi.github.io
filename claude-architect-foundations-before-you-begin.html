Booking & eligibility

Partner-company email required; candidates must be 18+. Register through Anthropic Partner Academy, then schedule with Pearson. Choose online proctoring or a test centre. Partner discounts may apply.

No mandatory certification prerequisite.

Exam-day checklist
  • Allow 135 minutes including check-in.
  • Bring government ID matching your booking.
  • Run Pearson’s system test for online delivery.
  • Closed book: no notes, documentation, or AI.
  • English only.
Changes & retakes

Change appointments at least 48 hours ahead. Retake waits: 14, 30, then 90 days; maximum four attempts per rolling year. Each retake is paid.
