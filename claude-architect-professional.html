01

Solution Design & Architecture

17%

Begin with business outcomes and measurable constraints. Compare a deterministic workflow, a retrieval-assisted application, and an autonomous agent against the same requirements. Make ownership and failure boundaries visible in the design.

Study action

Compare two architectures for a service-request assistant using latency, cost, reliability, and approval requirements. Explain which trade-offs you would accept.

02

Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering

13%

Choose models and context strategies using representative tasks and measured outcomes. Consider input quality, reasoning demands, latency, and cost together. A larger context window does not remove the need for relevance and evidence tracking.

Study action

Define an experiment comparing two model configurations on the same tasks. Specify quality criteria, latency measurements, and a cost budget.

03

Integration

19%

Design interfaces around data ownership, authorization, and failure recovery. Separate read operations from consequential writes. Use appropriate validation, idempotency, and retry policies at service boundaries rather than relying on prompts alone.

Study action

Design a RAG assistant that reads internal documents and creates service tickets. Show where retrieval permissions, approval, and duplicate prevention apply.

04

Evaluation, Testing & Optimization

16%

Build a representative evaluation set before optimizing. Break results down by task and failure type, and combine automated checks with human review where judgment matters. Re-test after changes to models, prompts, retrieval, or tools.

Study action

Create an evaluation matrix covering answer quality, groundedness, tool selection, latency, and cost. Define the evidence required to release a change.

05

Governance, Safety & Risk Management

14%

Identify sensitive data, risky actions, and accountable owners. Enforce access, retention, and approval policies outside the model. Consider prompt injection and unintended disclosure in the context of actual data flows and permissions.

Study action

Threat-model an assistant that processes confidential documents. Define preventive controls, audit events, and escalation paths for the highest-impact failures.

06

Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management

14%

Translate technical results into business consequences and explicit decisions. Agree on acceptance criteria, operating responsibilities, and feedback loops. Plan for model changes, incidents, rollback, and retirement throughout the application lifecycle.

Study action

Write a release recommendation that includes measured benefits, unresolved risks, ownership, and rollback criteria for a business sponsor.

07

Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement

7%

Give teams reusable patterns, useful observability, and clear operating guidance. Standardize common practices while preserving project-specific checks. Measure whether the tooling helps teams deliver reliable results rather than only increasing generated output.

Study action

Draft an operating checklist covering run traces, tool failures, cost trends, incident response, and how teams share improvements.
